miaonet_oobe_login_header= Login
miaonet_oobe_login_desc=
	Open the Mod options,
	find MiaoNet and press "Login" to start logging in.
	The game opens your browser on the authorization page;
	press "Authorize", and the page telling you authorization 
	is ready means you are logged in.
	You have to authorize within two minutes, on the same device.
miaonet_oobe_login_local=
	This version does not use forum authorization.
	Fill in your name and other info in the Mod options first,
	then turn on "Connected" below.

miaonet_oobe_connect_header= Connecting
miaonet_oobe_connect_desc=
	Back in the game, turn on "Connected" to connect to the server.
	"Connected" in the bottom left with the
	server welcome message means you are in.
	Enable "Connect on game start" below
	to connect on every game start.

miaonet_oobe_playerlist_header= Player List
miaonet_oobe_playerlist_desc=
	Press | to open the player list,
	which shows the state of everyone on the server;
	press it again to close.
	Use | and | to scroll the list.


miaonet_oobe_chat_header= Chat
miaonet_oobe_chat_desc=
	Press | to open the chat,
	where you can read the message history
	and start typing right away.
	Use | and | to scroll the message history.
	Press | to send, or | to discard
	and close the chat (your draft is not saved).
	The history starts when you connect,
	so you cannot read messages from before,
	and an unexpected disconnect clears it.

miaonet_oobe_command_header= Commands
miaonet_oobe_command_desc=
	Type "/" in the chat to start a command;
	a completion list pops up,
	pick with the arrow keys
	and press Tab to complete.
	Type "/help" to list every available command.
	You can also bind an "Input Commands" key
	to open the chat and start typing at once.

miaonet_oobe_players_header= Players in the Map
miaonet_oobe_players_desc=
	Other players in the same map are shown in game,
	and they see you as well.
	Their names are shown above their heads;
	if they get in the way,
	you can lower the opacity of players
	and player names in the options.

miaonet_oobe_emote_shortcut_header= Emote Shortcuts
miaonet_oobe_emote_shortcut_desc=
	Press 1 ~ 8 in a map to send the emote bound to that key;
	everyone in the map sees it.
	The "Send Emote 1 ~ 8" keys can be changed in the options.

miaonet_oobe_emote_command_header= Text Emotes
miaonet_oobe_emote_command_desc=
	Use "/emote text", or "/e text" for short, to send a text emote.
	Completions pop up here as well.

miaonet_oobe_emote_file_header= Custom Emotes
miaonet_oobe_emote_file_desc=
	The emote bound to each of the 1 ~ 8 keys can be changed too.
	That means editing the emotes file of this mod and reloading the emote settings.

miaonet_oobe_teleport_header= Teleport
miaonet_oobe_teleport_desc=
	"/teleport player", or "/tp player" for short, teleports you to that player.
	"/random-teleport", or "/rtp", picks a random player as the target.
	"/back" returns you to the room you were in, at one of its spawn points.
	If the other player is in a modded map, you need that mod installed and enabled to teleport to them.
	Players taking a golden strawberry are never picked by random teleport, but can still be targeted on purpose.

miaonet_oobe_watch_header= Watching
miaonet_oobe_watch_desc=
	"/watch player", or "/wt player", spectates that player.
	"/unwatch", or "/uw", stops watching and places you at a spawn point of
	the room the watched player is in. You can only watch players in the same map;
	while watching you disappear from the map for everyone else.

miaonet_oobe_mention_header= Mentions & Whispers
miaonet_oobe_mention_desc=
	Mention: typing "@player" in a message also plays a sound for that player.
	Whisper: "/whisper player message", or "/w player message", sends a private message.
	Completions pop up for commands and player names: pick with | and |, confirm with |.

miaonet_oobe_interactions_header= Player Interactions
miaonet_oobe_interactions_desc=
	With player interactions on, you and other players can bounce off and throw each other.
	This heavily affects normal play for both sides, so only turn it on when you need it.

miaonet_oobe_channel_header= Channels
miaonet_oobe_channel_desc=
	Channels work like rooms in other multiplayer games:
	in the same map, players in the same channel can see each other,
	and others cannot. Use "/channel name" or "/join name" to join
	a channel, creating it if it does not exist. The default main
	channel is called "main"; "/join main" takes you back to it.
	You cannot see the map other channels' players are in,
	cannot watch them and cannot pick them as a teleport
	target, but you can still mention or whisper to them.

miaonet_oobe_channel_private_header= Private Channels
miaonet_oobe_channel_tip=
	A channel whose name starts with a half-width "!" is private.
	Players outside cannot see the name of a private channel,
	but if they learn it and type it exactly, they can still join;
	the name they type must start with "!" as well. 
	Players inside a private channel can see the 
	current channel name in the player list,
	so mind your screenshots and avoid leaking it by accident.

miaonet_oobe_chat_scopes_header= Chat Scopes
miaonet_oobe_chat_scopes_desc=
	Chat scopes: the server has "Global", "Channel"
	and "Map" scopes, and the chat has an
	extra "All" tab holding every message you received.
	Switch tabs with | + | / |.
	A message sent from the "All" tab goes to the "Global" scope.
	Join / leave messages, server announcements
	and whispers show up in every tab.
	You can also send a single message to a specific scope:
	"/global-chat" or "/gc", "/channel-chat" or "/cc",
	"/map-chat" or "/mc".

miaonet_oobe_photo_header= Group Photo Mode
miaonet_oobe_photo_desc=
	When taking a group photo with many players,
	group photo mode is strongly recommended
	for easier positioning and lower server bandwidth:
	use "/group-photo-mode", or "/hy" for short, to enable it.
	A platform then appears in the picture and follows your mouse.
	Click | to pin the platform and teleport to its center;
	once pinned, | hides the platform and | unpins it.
	Other players never see your platform
	and will just see you floating in the air.
	While standing on the platform you cannot
	collect red strawberries, so you can carry one.

miaonet_oobe_fireworks_header= Fireworks
miaonet_oobe_photo_tip=
	Enable the "Fireworks" option below and bind "Create Fireworks",
	then press | to launch fireworks.
	Fireworks heavily affect other players' normal play,
	so only use them when it fits (e.g. a photo party).

miaonet_oobe_goldening_protection_header= Golden Protection
miaonet_oobe_goldening_protection_desc=
	Golden protection: while carrying a golden strawberry or similar challenge berry,
	you are never picked by random teleport until you finish it or lose the berry.
	Picking up such a berry also turns player interactions off automatically;
	turn them back on manually if you still want them.

miaonet_oobe_livemode_header= Live Mode
miaonet_oobe_livemode_desc=
	Live mode blocks every player's messages and emotes and stops you from sending your own;
	in the player list it hides the room name of every player (including yourself), and if
	other players are in modded maps you do not have, their map names are hidden as well.
	You still receive join / leave messages (which can be turned off separately) and announcements.
	Unlike other games, live mode does not hide nicknames or avatars, and it is never saved.

miaonet_oobe_interface_header= Interface
miaonet_oobe_interface_desc=
	If the player list and chat text look too small, tune them in the "UI" submenu below.
	To get close to the 0.4.1-alpha5 look, set "Player List UI Scale" and "Chat UI Scale" to 10,
	and "Chat Message Padding Height" to 2.

miaonet_oobe_finish_header= That's It!
miaonet_oobe_finish_desc=
	This tour only covers the basics; plenty more is not explained here
	(rich text messages and custom emotes, for example), so check the advanced guides.
	You can always use "/help" to list every available command.
	Have fun on MiaoNet!
